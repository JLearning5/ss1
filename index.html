Explore the pathways

These are examples from Nigeria’s revised curriculum. Ask your school which subjects and combinations it offers.

✳

Science

Explore how living things, materials and machines work.

Example subjects

  • Biology
  • Chemistry
  • Physics
  • Further Mathematics
Try it and look ahead

Try observing how two plants grow in different conditions and recording what changes. Related areas include health, engineering and agriculture.

✎

Arts (Humanities)

Explore stories, creativity, communities and how societies work.

Example subjects

  • Literature in English
  • Government
  • Visual Arts
  • Music
Try it and look ahead

Interview someone about a local issue and turn what you learn into a short article, drawing or debate. Related areas include law, media and design.

▤

Commercial (Business)

Explore money, trade and how businesses meet people’s needs.

Example subjects

  • Accounting
  • Commerce
  • Economics
  • Marketing
Try it and look ahead

Make a simple budget for a school event. Work out the cost of each item and where you could save money. Related areas include accounting, marketing and entrepreneurship.

+

Core and trade subjects

Students also take core subjects such as English Language and General Mathematics. The revised curriculum lists one trade subject alongside the core subjects. Ask your school which practical subjects it offers, such as garment making, computer hardware and phone repairs, or crop production.
